## 5. Ukraine drops Kerch Bridge arch (Europe / war)
- Ukrainian drones and missiles struck the Kerch Bridge linking Russia to
  occupied Crimea early Tuesday; the road arch and a span collapsed into
  the Kerch Strait, severing the road link (rail standing, arch damaged).
  Second straight day of strikes on the bridge; Zelenskyy called it an
  "unprecedented" operation and retaliation for Russian strikes on
  Ukrainian infrastructure.
- Russia pounded bridges in Kyiv in reply; two people were killed in the
  bridge strike, per Crimea's Russian-installed governor.

## 6. U.S. loses measles-free status (health)
- The Pan American Health Organization announced on Tuesday 17 Nov that the
  United States has lost its measles elimination status after 26 years
  (held since 2000), because measles circulated uninterrupted for over a
  year. Mexico and Bolivia lost the status the same day.
- CDC confirmed 5,412 U.S. measles cases in 2026 (as of 12 Nov), the highest
  in more than 30 years.
